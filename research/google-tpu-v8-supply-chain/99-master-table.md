## TPU "purity" trade structure

- **Pure TPU plays (TPU > NVDA exposure):** AVGO, CLS. These are the two names that should benefit asymmetrically from a Google capex acceleration.
- **TPU + NVDA both lift (CoWoS / advanced-package volume names):** TSM, AMKR, FORM, GLW, COHR, LITE — accelerator-agnostic at the bottleneck. Long-thesis works regardless of mix.
- **NVDA-skewed (TPU is small extra):** MPWR, AEIS, JBL, APH, TEL, VRT, MOD, NVT — long these for the broader AI capex, not specifically TPU.
- **Negligible TPU exposure (would be wrongly bucketed as TPU plays):** AAOI, CRDO, FLEX (power-only). The original supply-chain map overstates these.

## Key data quality flags

1. **MOD STALE**: Q4 FY26 reports 2026-05-27. Numbers are from Dec-25 quarter.
2. **TPU/NVDA splits are ESTIMATED for 18 of 22 names.** Only AVGO, TSM, CLS, VRT, NVT have any TPU-specific disclosure or naming. Others are inferred from accelerator-capex weighting.
3. **No company in this set discloses Google as a named >10% customer** in current filings. CLS comes closest (35% customer widely understood to be Google but not formally named).
4. **NVDA equity stakes distort optics for COHR, LITE, MRVL** (NVDA $2B preferred each). NVDA is now both customer and shareholder for these three — share-buying motivation is structurally different.
5. **Forward revenue confidence:** company-issued FY guides are credible for AVGO, TSM, VRT, CLS, AEIS, NVT, MPWR (all raised in last print). MOD, LITE, COHR mid-range. AAOI, CRDO, AMKR most uncertain.

## Trade ideas (preliminary, not investment advice)

- **TPU-pure long basket:** AVGO, CLS — the only names where TPU > NVDA share. Higher convexity to GOOG capex.
- **AI-bottleneck barbell (CoWoS + leading-edge node):** TSM core; AMKR speculative satellite. Wins regardless of TPU vs NVDA mix.
- **Optical TPU-attach pair:** Long LITE/COHR (200G EML lock-in to TPU v8 fabric) vs short AAOI (excluded from GOOG 800G per TrendForce). All three have similar headline AI growth — the divergence in TPU exposure is the alpha.
- **CDU thermal spread:** Long VRT or NVT (Deschutes-named) vs short MOD (absent from Deschutes vendor list despite product launch). Mean-reverts only if MOD is added later — asymmetric.
- **Power "Blackwell-overhang lifts" mean revert:** MPWR enterprise-data +98% YoY confirms share recovery; if FY26 hits enterprise-data growth target the de-rate corrects.
- **Don't pay TPU multiple for:** CRDO, AAOI, FLEX (TPU exposure ~0). Currently mis-priced as TPU adjacent but core revenue is elsewhere.
